Our Story

Founded on a simple idea

In 2009, we watched too many HVAC companies run on guesswork — technicians guessing at refrigerant levels, quoting repairs without diagnostics, and leaving customers with surprise invoices.

We built AERION on the opposite principle: measure first, fix second. Every service call starts with a full system readout. Every quote comes with a written report. Every technician carries thermal cameras and digital manometers — not just wrenches.

Today, we maintain over 2,400 systems across the metro area — residential, commercial, and industrial. Our maintenance plan clients average 18% lower energy bills and 73% fewer emergency calls.

Operating Principles

How we work

Measure, Don't Guess

Every diagnosis starts with data — temperature differentials, airflow rates, refrigerant pressures, and electrical readings. We don't guess; we measure, then explain.

Transparent Quoting

You get a written report with findings and options before any work begins. No surprise invoices, no hidden fees, no pressure. Just honest recommendations.

Certified Technicians

Every technician is EPA-certified, manufacturer-trained, and carries a full diagnostic toolkit. We invest in ongoing training — your system deserves current expertise.

24/7 Availability

Climate emergencies don't wait for business hours. Our dispatch operates around the clock — average response time of 38 minutes, no overtime charges.
